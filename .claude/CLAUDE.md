## LearnForge

## Goal

A developer-focused learning platform designed to document and share the journey of learning, building, and creating.

The platform brings together structured course-wise learning notes, technical blogs, real-world projects, resources, and personal learning experiences in one place. It is built around the idea of learning by doing—turning concepts into practical projects and sharing the knowledge and progress publicly.

The goal is to create an open, growing knowledge platform where developers can explore learning materials, understand technical concepts, discover projects, and follow real learning journeys while contributing their own knowledge and work.

## Technology Stack

Database: PostgresSQL
Plugin: Sequelize
Backend: Node.js
Backend: Framework Nest.js
Frontend: Next.js
Styling Framework: CSS - TailwindCSS
